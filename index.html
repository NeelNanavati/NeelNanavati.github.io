Twin Deficits: United States, Germany & Japan

Does a larger government deficit go together with a larger current account deficit? I merged IMF and BEA data from 1980 to 2024 and tested the hypothesis with OLS in levels and first differences, Newey-West standard errors, COVID-year controls, rolling correlations, ADF unit-root tests and Phillips-Ouliaris cointegration tests.

The short answer: the evidence is weak and mixed. The relationship looks strong for Germany in levels, but it disappears once you account for trending series.
